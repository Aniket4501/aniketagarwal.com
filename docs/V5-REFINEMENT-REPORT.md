## 3. The voice edit

**Cut from 38 instances to 10.** The phrase "I would rather X than Y" alone went from 7 to 1.

**What was cut** — sentences about the placement of the sentence they were in:
- *"I would rather state both than pick whichever one flatters me"* → *"Both are true."*
- *"That is not modesty. Bounding a claim is what makes the unbounded ones worth anything."*

**What was kept** — caveats carrying information a reader can act on: the Grounded v1 degenerate baseline, the labels that moved toward the harness, where the step-sync denominator stops, "I built the artifact; I did not own the sale", the north-star correction.

**The three unqualified claims:**
1. **Step Syncing** — *"The app bundle went from 25MB to 6MB, a 76% reduction. Launch time went from fifteen seconds to under two."*
2. **Steps Premier League** — *"Three retention strategies were evaluated. One shipped, and it shipped from nothing."*

A reviewer's fair challenge: the bundle number was *escorted* five times by the phrase "two stated totals", which is qualification by repetition. That is now down to one occurrence.

**Cross-page repetition turned out to be the stronger evidence of a device**, and it was invisible from any single page. The same twenty-word sentence appeared on two pages with one preposition swapped; *"A decision with no cost was not a decision"* ran verbatim on three; the cohort-analysis line on three. ## 9. Disposition or device?

**The VP's verdict: still a device — and the edit fixed the wrong layer.**

Their argument, which I think is right: the hedges were a symptom. The formula is (a) a fixed structural schedule — seven of nine long pages still end in a designated confession slot, so by the second case study you can predict the last section of every page you have not opened — and (b) one sentence shape, *X is not Y, it is Z*, which runs 25 times across the site.

Their diagnostic is the useful part: **does the concession end, or does it get a return?** Where an admission is followed by a recovery clause that converts it into a demonstration, it reads as device. Where it just stops — Grounded's *"That set does not exist yet."*, *"That is the obvious next piece and it is not built."*, the dated Strava correction — it reads as disposition, because it is unrecoverable as a strength.

**What I did about it:** cut the three sentences that name the technique out loud, and de-duplicated across pages. **What I did not do:** dismantle the section schedule. Removing "What I would do differently" from three case studies is a structural rewrite, not a refinement, and this round was explicitly scoped against that. It is the right next move and it belongs to a decision you should make deliberately.

## 10. The single highest-value thing still missing

**Unchanged from last round, and now named by three independent reviewers: one decision where a named person disagreed with you, and what happened.**

The Head of Product put it most precisely. Every correction on this site is internal — the rules disagreed with the labels, the denominators disagreed with each other, the stock disagreed with the flow. **Not once does a person disagree with him.** That is a closed loop, and the site diagnoses it itself on `/approach`: *"What this artifact is missing: who objected."*

The question it still cannot survive: *tell me about a time a user, or a colleague, said something that changed your mind.* Two sentences close it. No amount of additional writing will.
