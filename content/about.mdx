One app, and most of what I know about product came out of it: a consumer
health product with 1M+ registered users, where I define the roadmap, write the
PRDs, and decide what ships in what order. In practice that means being the
person who has to be right about what gets built next, and then being specific
about which number would show whether I was.

Before HCL there were three months at Circle Health on the claims journey and,
in early 2023, a stint at Infinyte Club where a five-step verification wall was
the reason people did not finish signing up. Both are written up here at three
hundred words each, which is about what either is worth. I finished a dual
degree at IIT Kharagpur in 2024 and have been doing product work since 2022.

**One limit, before you find it yourself: I have never owned acquisition.** The
distribution at HCL is inherited. The users were already arriving when I got
there, through a channel I did not build, and every number I can show you sits
downstream of that — launch time, session time, daily actives, what somebody
does once they are already inside the product. I have never had to defend a cost
per acquisition or run a paid channel. If that is the job, I am not yet the
person for it.

What I am good at is the layer under it: finding the reason a product people
have already been handed is not being used, and saying out loud what the fix can
and cannot be credited with.

I am looking for PM, APM or AI PM work in consumer product, health, and applied
AI. The fastest way to reach me is email, and I answer it.
