I work on consumer health product at HCL Healthcare, where I own engagement on an app with more
than a million registered users — the roadmap, the PRDs, and what ships in what order. My title
there is Product Analyst. The scope is a PM’s, and I would rather state both than pick whichever
one flatters me.

I got here sideways. A dual degree at IIT Kharagpur, then four product internships across media,
online marketplaces, fintech onboarding and insurance claims, each of which was mostly an exercise in
finding out why people stopped partway through something. That turned out to be the job.

**The problems I like are the ones where the product already exists and something quiet is stopping
people from using it.** A fifteen-second wait. A five-step verification wall in front of an account
that does not exist yet. A page of clinical values nobody can read. Those are more interesting to me
than a blank page, because the constraint is real and the evidence is already there if you go and
look for it.

Health is where I want to keep working. It has the hardest version of the engagement problem —
people have very little intrinsic reason to open a health app on a Tuesday — and the consequences of
getting it right are not trivial.

I’m looking for PM, APM or AI PM roles in consumer product, health and applied AI.
