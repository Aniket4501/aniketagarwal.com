I owned discovery for the claims journey and mapped it end to end, which is how the drop-off points
stopped being anecdotal. Then I built a real-time dashboard tracking 50K+ user journeys, so the
question in sprint planning changed from *where do we think people leave* to *here is where they
leave.*

The redesign resequenced the flow rather than shortening it. **A claim cannot ask for less — it can
only ask in a better order**, front-loading what a person already has to hand and deferring what
they need to go and find.

Alongside it I ran year-on-year claims analysis across 20+ enterprise clients, which gave the
commercial team something concrete to take into renewal conversations.
